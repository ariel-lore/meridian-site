# Proof points: honest early-stage system for Meridian Virtual Bookkeeping

**Purpose:** Grow social proof without inventing metrics, fake reviews, logos, or customer counts.  
**Audience:** Bart (approve claims) · Techie (place approved microcopy) · future case studies.  
**Rule:** If it is not true today, it stays a template or a ladder step. Never ship brackets or placeholders as live claims.

|-------|----------------|----------------------|

## Proof ladder

Climb one rung at a time. Only move copy up when the evidence exists.

### Week 0 (now)

- Process clarity on every bookkeeping page  
- Boundaries and pricing “from” language  
- Fit-check SLA + trust strip  
- Hero proof Variant A (studio / process)  
- Soft ask to existing Meridian clients (see `13-existing-client-soft-offer.md`) for optional testimonials later  

**Site line:**  
`Meridian studio · Async written monthly close · Clear scope, hard admin cap`

### After 3 clients (paying, books actually closed)

- Anonymized outcome line with real `[N]` (e.g. “3”) and vague industry if needed (“early-stage software”)  
- 1–2 short founder quotes with permission (name optional; role + company type OK)  
- One filled case-study template (even one page)  
- Optional: “Written close delivered by the [Nth] of the month” if that is contractually true  

**Site line pattern:**  
`Closed and reconciled books for [N] [industry] teams. Written monthly report.`

### After 10 clients

- Stronger anonymized aggregate (“10+ technical teams”) only if true  
- 3 case studies on a `/virtual-bookkeeping/proof/` or resources section  
- CPA partner quote if a partner will put it in writing  
- Directory listings (QBO/Xero) once profiles are live and accurate  

### Review sites (when you have enough volume and willingness)

- Google Business Profile reviews (real only)  
- Clutch / similar only if profiles are maintained and responses are professional  
- Never buy reviews. Never seed fake stars on the marketing site.

## What NEVER to invent

- Customer counts (“47 SaaS teams,” “100+ founders”)  
- Fake star ratings or review widgets with made-up scores  
- Logo walls without written permission  
- Invented revenue, runway, or “hours saved” averages  
- Fake CPA or founder quotes  
- “Featured in” or award badges that are not real  
- Claiming to be a CPA, enrolled agent, or tax filer  
- Promising weekly meetings, full EA cover, or unlimited admin  
- Competitor smear (call categories honestly; do not call peers scams)  
- Shipping Variant B/C hero lines with unfilled `[N]`, `[industry]`, or `[CPA name]`
